### Two modes

**Diff** — the lines you changed, since the last completed review or since a
ref you pick. This is what the commit-time warning always measures.

**Reading** — every line of every file you open. For learning a codebase, or
reviewing someone else's branch. Needs a folder, not a repository.

**Auto** (the default) picks diff inside a repository with changes, and reading
everywhere else.

A folder you opened by accident does not have to stay in the denominator:
**Stop Measuring This File** takes it out, evidence and all.
