### What the report answers

**How much of this change have you actually read?**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Review coverage | the share of changed lines your attention rested on |
| Blindspot | the rest — what goes in unread |
| Worst hunk | the unread range with the highest risk |

Coverage is estimated from IDE events alone: which line the caret and the
viewport centre held, for how long, and how much there was to read there.
No eye tracker, and nothing leaves your machine.

Default shortcut: `Ctrl+Alt+B` (`Cmd+Alt+B` on macOS).
